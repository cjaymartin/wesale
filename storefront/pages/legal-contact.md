# Contact, Privacy & Terms (paste into Shopify pages)

> Shopify auto-generates Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Refund Policy templates under **Settings → Policies** — use those generators and paste your specifics. The notes below are what to fill in. This is operational guidance, not legal advice; have a lawyer review before scaling.

## Contact (/pages/contact)
**Get in touch** — Questions about an order, sizing, or your nosey mutt? Email **hello@noseymutt.com** and we'll reply within 24 hours (Mon–Fri).
- Add a simple contact form (Shopify Dawn has a built-in `Contact form` section).
- Business contact: hello@noseymutt.com
- (Optional, builds trust) a mailing address — a USPS PO box or virtual address is fine to start.

## Privacy Policy (Settings → Policies → generate)
Fill in:
- Data collected: name, email, shipping address, payment info (via Shopify Payments/PayPal — we never store card numbers), browsing/analytics (Meta Pixel, Google Analytics).
- Why: to fulfill orders, provide support, send marketing (with consent), and improve the store.
- Third parties: Shopify, payment processors, our fulfillment partner (CJ/DSers), email/marketing tools, ad pixels.
- Rights: how to opt out of marketing and request data deletion (hello@noseymutt.com).
- Cookie/pixel consent banner (use a free consent app; required for Meta/EU traffic).

## Terms of Service (Settings → Policies → generate)
Standard Shopify template covers it. Confirm it includes: pricing/availability subject to change, order acceptance/cancellation rights, limitation of liability, and **a supervision/safety note** (enrichment products must be used under supervision; not indestructible chew toys).
