# Homepage copy & section plan — NoseyMutt

Build order top → bottom in the Shopify theme editor (Dawn theme, free).

## 1. Announcement bar
`🐽 Free US shipping over $35 · 30-day happy-dog guarantee`

## 2. Hero (image/video banner)
- **Background:** short looping clip or photo of a dog working a snuffle/slow-feeder bowl (use supplier media or your own sample-order footage — own footage converts best).
- **Headline:** Keep their nose busy, their brain happy.
- **Subhead:** Enrichment gear that slows fast eaters and busts boredom — so your dog is calmer and your couch survives.
- **Primary CTA:** Shop the Starter Kit → `/products/boredom-buster-starter-kit`
- **Secondary CTA:** Shop all enrichment → `/collections/the-enrichment-collection`

## 3. "Sound familiar?" problem strip (3 icons)
- 🍽️ **Inhales dinner in 9 seconds** → Slow-Feeder Bowl
- 🛋️ **Bored & wrecking the house** → Snuffle + Puzzle toys
- 😬 **Can't relax for baths/alone-time** → Calm-Down Lick Mat

## 4. Featured product — the Starter Kit (the hero)
- Image + the kit hook + "$59 (save $10)" + Add to Cart.
- 3 thumbnails of the included items.

## 5. "Why enrichment?" trust band (3 columns)
- **Sniffing tires dogs out** — 10 minutes of foraging beats a long walk for mental energy.
- **Slower eating, happier tummy** — no more gulp-and-bloat.
- **A busy dog is a calm dog** — less chewing, barking, and digging from boredom.
> Keep it benefit-framed. No medical claims.

## 6. The Enrichment Collection grid
- Pull the collection: bowl, lick mat, snuffle toy, puzzle, travel bowl.

## 7. Social proof
- Reviews widget (see setup checklist — use a free reviews app, seed with sample-order photos/UGC, never fake testimonials).
- "As loved by nosey mutts everywhere 🐾" UGC strip once you have it.

## 8. Guarantee + shipping clarity
- **30-day happy-dog guarantee** — if your dog's not more entertained, we'll make it right.
- **Shipping:** clearly state realistic delivery window (see `pages/shipping-policy.md`). Honest ETAs = fewer angry emails + ad-policy safe.

## 9. Email capture
- "Get 10% off + a free enrichment-ideas guide." Captures emails for the LTV/refill remarketing the strategy needs.

## 10. Footer
- Links: Shipping, Returns, Contact, FAQ, Privacy, Terms. Social icons (@noseymutt).
