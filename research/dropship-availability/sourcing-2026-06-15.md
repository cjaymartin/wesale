# Dropship Supplier Availability — 2026-06-15

**Agent:** Supplier Sourcing · **Market:** US · **Budget:** $500 hard cap
**Mandate:** Verify real supplier availability, landed cost, shipping, MOQ, ratings, and storefront integration for 3 shortlisted products. Compute realistic gross margin at a sane US retail. Flag fulfillment/IP risk. Give viable/marginal/reject verdicts.

## Method, confidence & data caveats

- Suppliers verified as **live and listed** on CJdropshipping, AliExpress, and Spocket via search + page fetch (June 2026). Links cited per row.
- **Exact unit cost + live shipping quote on AliExpress and CJ require a logged-in account** — both platforms redirect anonymous fetches to a login/validation wall, and CJ throws "too many attempts at shipping calculation" without a session. Where I could not pull an exact logged-in landed cost, I give a **sourced estimate** (from the visible listing price, secondary price aggregators, or Spocket's disclosed cost) and **flag it [SAMPLE-NEEDED]**. These must be confirmed with a real test order before any ad spend.
- **Spocket** disclosed exact cost→retail→margin on its public dropship listings (most reliable hard data we have). Spocket skews to US/EU suppliers = faster shipping but higher cost.
- Retail anchors are from live Amazon best-seller / category data (cited).
- Gross margin = (retail − landed cost) / retail, where landed cost = unit cost + per-unit shipping. Ad cost (CPA) is NOT included here — that is the next gate (strategy targets landed ≤30–35% of retail to leave room for ~3× markup + ads).

## Recommendation

### 🏆 HERO: Slow-feeder / enrichment dog bowls
Best margin × reliability on the board. Deep, redundant supplier pool across CJ, AliExpress, AND Spocket (no single-supplier dependency), no MOQ, ~70–83% gross on the China route with a Spocket US-warehouse fast-ship fallback, frictionless DSers/CJ integration, rising evergreen pet demand, and no IP or health-claim landmine. Strongest scorecard triangle (Margin + Supplier + Demand). Avoid ceramic SKUs (breakage/weight); lead with silicone/plastic + lick-mat bundle.

### 🥈 BACKUP: Car seat gap filler (cup-holder / organizer tray form factor)
Equivalent margin (~68–80% gross) and a far stronger impulse demo, held to backup because of two gating risks: the **Drop Stop patent** (must ship the organizer-tray style, not the wedge, and avoid the brand name) and a more saturated field. De-risk both and it can overtake the hero on conversion. Natural to bundle Product 3 (headrest hooks) with it to lift AOV.

**Headrest hooks → not a standalone hero; deploy as an AOV/bundle attach to the gap filler.**
