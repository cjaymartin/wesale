## CRITICAL: Single-Parcel Analysis — "Boredom-Buster Starter Kit" (Bowl + Lick Mat + Snuffle)

**Question:** Can the kit ship as ONE parcel, or is it 3 separate shipments from 3 suppliers?

**Findings:**
1. **Different AliExpress stores ⇒ 3 parcels, guaranteed.** The verified best-rated SKUs above live in *different* AliExpress storefronts (the bowl, the $3.32 lick mat, and the $6.33–$8.33 snuffle mat are separate sellers). DSers fulfills each line from its own seller → **3 separate packages, 3 tracking numbers, 3 staggered ETAs.** This is the margin/CSAT killer the brand report flagged.
2. **CJdropshipping CAN consolidate into ONE parcel — this is the fix.** CJ confirms that multiple CJ products in one customer order **ship as a single consolidated parcel when fulfilled from the same CJ warehouse**, and CJ also supports manual order-combining (combined orders get an "HB" order number) ([CJ: How to Combine Orders for the Same Receiver](https://blog.cjdropshipping.com/detail/1331174490536628225), [CJ help](https://cjdropshipping.com/article-details/158)). CJ carries **all three** categories — slow-feeder bowls (verified in `sourcing-2026-06-15.md`), lick mats ([CJ Pet Mats](https://cjdropshipping.com/list/wholesale-pet-mats-l-2410110357391611900.html)), and snuffle balls/mats ([foldable ball](https://cjdropshipping.com/product/foldable-dog-snuffle-ball-dog-training-toys-increase-iq-pet-cat-training-games-feeding-intelligence-toy-p-1509105077167476736.html), [carrot mat](https://cjdropshipping.com/product/pet-dog-toys-carrot-plush-toy-vegetable-chew-toy-for-dogs-snuffle-mat-for-dogs-cats-durable-chew-puppy-toy-dogs-accessories-p-1455000172097572864.html)).
3. **Caveat — same-warehouse is the condition.** Consolidation only holds if all 3 chosen CJ SKUs sit in the **same CJ warehouse** (ideally the CJ US warehouse for 3–6 day ETA). If one SKU is China-only and another is US-WH, CJ splits them. This must be confirmed per-SKU at sample time. [SAMPLE-NEEDED: confirm all 3 kit SKUs share one CJ warehouse.]

**RECOMMENDATION:** **Source the entire Boredom-Buster Starter Kit (bowl + lick mat + snuffle) from CJdropshipping, single supplier, same warehouse — ideally CJ's US warehouse.** This converts 3 parcels into 1, protects the ETA and review profile, and removes the multi-tracking CX mess. Accept a slightly higher CJ unit cost vs. the cheapest AliExpress promo as the price of single-parcel fulfillment — margins above (64–88% gross) have ample room to absorb it. Keep AliExpress/DSers only for **standalone single-SKU** orders (where 1 line = 1 parcel anyway) and as a backup supplier pool. **Do NOT fulfill the kit across AliExpress + CJ + Spocket — that is the 3-parcel trap.**
