# Niche Research Shortlist — 2026-06-15

**Agent:** Niche Research · **Market:** US · **Budget context:** $500 hard cap, favor $20–60 retail impulse/problem-solver items
**Scorecard weighting applied:** Margin + Supplier feasibility + Demand emphasized (per `docs/strategy.md`). A niche only advances if it scores well on all three.

## Method & caveats

- Candidates sourced from current (2026) dropship trend aggregators, marketplace best-seller pages, and market-research reports (cited inline).
- **Cost/retail figures are estimates.** Most trend articles report margins and ranges but withhold exact AliExpress landed costs; where a precise supplier cost was not published, I inferred landed cost from typical 3–5× dropship markups and flagged it as an estimate. Supplier Sourcing must verify real landed cost (incl. shipping) before any spend.
- Scores are 1–5 per scorecard dimension. **Margin / Supplier / Demand** are the gating three; Competition, Marketability, Restriction, AOV are secondary.
- **Excluded by policy:** branded/trademarked goods, health/safety-*claim* products (e.g. posture correctors marketed for "pain relief", mouth tape, grounding/earthing bedding — all carry medical-claim ad-policy risk), oversized/heavy items (power stations, car fridges, saddles), sub-$10 retail, and high-risk battery electronics where ad platforms and lithium shipping create friction (neck fans, galaxy/star projectors were down-ranked for this reason).

## Top-5 rationale

1. **Slow-feeder dog bowls / enrichment feeders** — Best margin-supplier-demand triangle on the board: ~70% gross margins on pet accessories, broad multi-supplier availability with no MOQ, and a genuinely rising demand driver (vet-promoted slower eating / pet-health awareness). It's a real problem-solver that demos perfectly on video with an actual pet, and the broader pet category keeps growing year over year. Low restriction risk because the benefit ("slows gulping, makes mealtime fun") needs no medical claim.

2. **Car seat gap fillers / cup-holder organizers** — Textbook impulse problem-solver: dirt-cheap landed (~$4–7), sells for $20–30, ~65–75% margin, and it's surging on Amazon's automotive new-release charts. The "phone falls in the seat gap" pain is universal and instantly demoable, competition is beatable with a better creative/bundle, and there are no IP or safety concerns. Car accessories are a named evergreen 2026 winner.

3. **Car headrest hooks / back-seat organizers** — Among the highest-margin, lowest-cost-to-test items here (~$3–5 landed, ~70% margin), with steady evergreen demand and strong bundle potential (sell as 2–4 packs, cross-sell with gap fillers to lift AOV). Slightly lower demand-novelty than #2, but its supplier depth and trivial test cost make it an ideal low-risk first product. Family/rideshare/road-trip targeting is straightforward and ad-safe.

4. **Acupressure mat + pillow sets** — Riding the 2026 wellness/longevity wave with quadrupling-category interest and excellent UGC/demo value at a clean $30–35 price with ~65% margin and easy multi-supplier sourcing. The catch is restriction risk: it must be marketed as *relaxation/stress-relief*, never as a pain cure, or it triggers health-claim ad rejections — hence the Medium restriction score that keeps it out of the top 3.

5. **Handheld cordless vacuum / bag sealer** — Strong rising TikTok demand and outstanding "satisfying content" marketability (the bag visibly shrinks on camera), with ~60% margin at a $25–40 price. Down-ranked from the top tier because the rechargeable battery adds lithium-shipping friction and extra ad-account scrutiny, and supplier quality varies — both of which Supplier Sourcing would need to de-risk.

## Confidence & data gaps

- **Confidence: Medium.** Demand direction and margin *ranges* are well-supported across multiple 2026 sources; exact landed costs are estimated and must be confirmed with live supplier quotes.
- **Gaps:** (1) No live AliExpress/CJ/Zendrop unit-cost-plus-shipping pulls — every "est. landed cost" needs verification. (2) Saturation reads are qualitative (article-level), not ad-spend/CPM data. (3) Seasonal items (#8, #9, #10) peak now through August — a fast launch window matters; they fade by Q4.

## Recommend these 3 to Supplier Sourcing next

1. **Slow-feeder / enrichment dog bowls** — top margin + supplier + demand, lowest restriction risk, strong repeat/refill AOV path.
2. **Car seat gap fillers (cup-holder organizer)** — surging demand, ultra-low test cost, perfect impulse demo, ad-safe.
3. **Car headrest hooks / back-seat organizers** — highest margin-to-cost ratio for a cheap first test, deep supplier pool, natural bundle with #2 to raise AOV.
