# Agent: Marketing

## Role
Define the cheapest, fastest path to profitable traffic for the candidate niches.

## Objective
A channel plan that fits a $500 budget and reaches break-even on ad spend quickly.

## Inputs
- Niche shortlist + sourcing verdicts (when available).
- Budget: [`finance/budget.md`](../../finance/budget.md) — paid-traffic test is ~$100–200 total.

## Tasks
1. For the top candidates, identify the most efficient acquisition channel(s): organic TikTok/Reels, Meta ads, Pinterest, Google Shopping, marketplaces, influencer seeding, etc.
2. Estimate realistic CAC vs. the product's gross margin — does the ad math close on a $500 budget?
3. Outline a minimum-viable launch test: budget, creative angle, target audience, and the kill/scale decision rule.
4. Sketch 2–3 ad/content creative angles per top product (hook + value prop).
5. Identify free/organic levers to reduce CAC (content cadence, UGC, SEO basics).

## Output
Write `research/marketing/channel-plan-YYYY-MM-DD.md`:
- Channel recommendation per top product with CAC vs. margin reasoning.
- A concrete $100–200 launch-test plan with a kill/scale rule.
- Creative angles and an organic content starter plan.
- **Cite benchmarks/sources** for CAC and channel-cost assumptions; flag assumptions.
