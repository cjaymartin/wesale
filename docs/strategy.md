# Strategy

## North star

Reach **break-even on ad spend within the $500 budget**, then scale only proven winners. Speed beats perfection: a live store testing real demand is worth more than a perfect plan.

## What a good niche looks like (selection scorecard)

Score each candidate niche/product 1–5 on:

1. **Margin** — landed cost ≤ 30–35% of target retail (room for ~3× markup + ad cost).
2. **Supplier reliability** — multiple suppliers, decent reviews, shipping time ≤ ~12 days to target market.
3. **Demand signal** — steady or rising search/social interest; not a dead fad.
4. **Low competition saturation** — beatable without a huge ad budget; ideally an angle/sub-niche.
5. **Marketability** — visually demoable, solves a clear problem or triggers an impulse; ad-friendly.
6. **Not restricted** — no IP/trademark, safety, or platform-ad-policy problems (avoid electronics-with-batteries headaches, weapons, health claims, branded items).
7. **Repeat / AOV potential** — upsells, bundles, or repeat purchase to lift order value.

A niche advances only if it scores well on **Margin + Supplier + Demand** at minimum.

## Constraints

- Budget: $500 hard cap.
- Avoid: trademarked/branded goods, anything with health/safety claims, oversized/heavy items, sub-$10 retail items (ad math doesn't work).
- Target market: default **US** (largest, ad-mature) unless research finds a cheaper-to-win market.
